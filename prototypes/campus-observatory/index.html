      What is real in this model?

        About this prototype

        A test of the look and the controls for a future Campus Health Observatory. It is separate from the HealthWiz app and does not read or change its data.

        Where the data comes from

        • Buildings, paths, fields, river: the OpenStreetMap export you supplied. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. It has no building heights, no terrain and no staircases.
        • Raised ground (Middle Elevation, Highest Elevation): outlines traced from your annotated screenshot. The heights are illustrative and only show the order: base, then middle, then highest. They are not measurements.
        • Staircase names, locations, angles, categories: the HealthWiz app's stair list. Their source was never recorded, so every location is an estimated location.
        • Step rise and run of ST14, ST19 and ST20: the Group 14 F21 report, Table 2 (tape, mean of 5 steps).
        • 3D staircase models and building heights: illustrative only, modelled in Blender. Their numbers are demonstration values, not measurements.
        • Lighting and shadows: a rendering effect. The light direction is chosen for a clear picture, not the real sun, so the shadows are not real shade on campus.
        • Fantasy buildings: the walls follow each real OpenStreetMap outline; the roofs, eaves, chimneys, corner turrets with spires and the Surau's dome are a fantasy treatment modelled in Blender on that outline. They are not the buildings' real shapes or heights.
        • Trees, lanterns, glows, particles, the dais: decoration. They mark nothing real.
        • You on the map (Tom): only after you tap the locate button and allow it, the character stands at your device's GPS position and walks as you move; the blue circle is the device's own accuracy estimate. The position stays in this browser tab: it is not saved or sent anywhere. Tom is drawn larger than life so he can be seen. "Demo walk" moves him along a campus road; that position is simulated, not yours. The character is from the model you supplied ("Tom"), reduced and given a walk in Blender.

        Labels you will see

          Settings

          Map: drag to move, pinch or scroll to zoom, two-finger twist to rotate, two-finger drag up or down to tilt. Keyboard: Tab to a staircase marker and press Enter; arrow keys move the map; + and − zoom.
