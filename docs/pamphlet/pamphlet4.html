THE QUEST

Health tracking is easy to start and hard to keep up. In HealthWiz Kingdom every healthy action has a visible effect.

You log water, meals, sleep, stairs (with workout heart rate), runs and stress. Medius, the Wizard King, guides you as your real actions restore the kingdom, with Luna, an apprentice witch, as your everyday companion.

How it works

1Log a real habit: a glass of water, a meal, a climb.
2Restore the kingdom: its regions heal as you keep going.
3Understand: a “?” shows the formula and sources behind every number.

What sets it apart

1Health first, game second. Game values (XP, levels, badges) are tagged “game progress, not a health measurement”.

2Every number shows its working: inputs, formula, your numbers, sources, limits.

3Honest about uncertainty. Values are labelled measured, calculated, estimated, simulated or missing. No invented readings.

4Private and optional. Works on your device with no account; an account is only for backup and sync.

TRACKFEATURES · the Health Hall

Water QuestDaily goal from your weight (35 mL/kg, the team’s own rule). The knight walks, drinks and pours as you log; opt-in live weather adjusts your water potion.

NutritionLog from the Kolej MARA Kulim canteen menu. Signed in, Luke searches the web for any food, with a source for every part and an energy check (4·protein + 4·carb + 9·fat). Estimates are labelled.

SleepHours against National Sleep Foundation ranges, a Sleep Score (our own game formula), Dream Battle, Counting Sheep and a wake-up alarm.

Stress QuestDASS-21 check-in (no severity labels), the Storm Within, Calming Grove breathing, five relief activities. Crisis numbers are always on screen.

Body & EnergyBMI (a screening number, not a diagnosis), BMR by Mifflin–St Jeor, daily energy = BMR × FAO/WHO/UNU activity level, macro and fibre goals.

Training Hall22 moves, 19 muscle maps; Calisthenics is bodyweight only. “I feel dizzy” and “Chest pain” stop buttons always at hand.

Running RoadGPS runs: distance, pace, calories (MET × kg × h, 2024 Compendium), opt-in Runners’ Board.

Stairs & heart rateBPM before, after and 1 minute after a climb for recovery. Camera pulse (video never stored) or a smartwatch / chest strap over Web Bluetooth.

EverywhereLog for yesterday or any earlier date, browse every entry by day, print a weekly report.

The Group 14 stair experimentSix participants climbed measured stairways. The pulse is modelled as periodic motion (f, T, ω) and VO₂ estimated with the project’s method. Automated tests recompute the report’s tables from the raw counts. If the pulse after is lower than before, the app says “not valid” instead of showing a number.

Evidence VaultEvery source on a shelf: claim, how HealthWiz uses it, where you see it. Every “?” links to its entry.

Science LaboratoryBe Your Own Experiment, the Evidence Trial, and the Uncertainty Engine showing how unmeasured assumptions move an estimate.

Ascent MonumentYour climbing builds an endless Tower of Babel, one stone per 0.25 m.

Alchemist’s WorkshopFood energy, work against gravity and metabolic use are three different quantities; it shows why.

TRUSTPrivate, honest, open

Your data stays yours

  • Saved on your device; download or restore a backup any time.
  • A free account is optional: cloud backup and sync. Row Level Security means only you can read your row.
  • Delete account and cloud data in two taps.
  • Location only when you tap, never saved or sent. Camera pulse video never stored.
  • AI chat is never stored; no AI key inside the app.

Under the hood

A static web app: plain JavaScript, no build step. Installable and offline-ready.
