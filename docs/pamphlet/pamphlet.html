THE QUEST

Health tracking is easy to start and hard to keep up. In HealthWiz Kingdom every healthy action has a visible effect.

It is a pixel-art fantasy RPG that is also a health tracker. You log water, meals, sleep, stairs (with workout heart rate), runs and stress. Medius, the Wizard King, guides you as your real actions restore the kingdom, with Luna, an apprentice witch, as your everyday companion.

How it works

1Loga real habit: a glass of water, a meal, a climb
2Restoreregions of the kingdom heal as you keep going
3Understanda “?” shows the formula and sources behind every number

What sets it apart

1Health first, game second. Health numbers and units stay in a plain, readable face. Game values (XP, levels, badges) are tagged “game progress, not a health measurement”.

2Every number shows its working. Inputs, formula, your own numbers step by step, linked sources and limits.

3Honest about uncertainty. Each value is labelled measured, calculated, estimated, simulated or missing. No invented readings, no made-up error margins.

4Private and optional. Works on your device with no account; an account is only for backup and sync.

Your data stays yours

  • Saved on your device. Download or restore a backup any time.
  • Free account is optional: cloud backup and sync. Row Level Security means only you can read your row.
  • Delete account and cloud data in two taps.
  • Location is read only when you tap, and is never saved or sent. Camera pulse video is never stored.
  • Medius AI chat is never stored, and there is no AI key inside the app.

TRACKThe Health Hall · eight trackers in one place

Water QuestYour daily goal comes from your weight (35 mL/kg: the team’s own rule, and the “?” says so). The knight walks, drinks, carries and pours as you log. Opt in to live weather and the Elemental Thermometer adjusts your water potion.

NutritionLog from the Kolej MARA Kulim canteen menu with calories. Signed in, Luke searches the web for any food and portion, shows a source for every part and checks the energy with 4·protein + 4·carb + 9·fat. All estimates are labelled.

SleepHours against National Sleep Foundation ranges, a Sleep Score (our own game formula, labelled as such), the Dream Battle, Counting Sheep and a wake-up alarm for Android, iPhone and calendar.

Stress QuestThe DASS-21 check-in (21 statements, three scales, no severity labels), the Storm Within, Calming Grove breathing and five relief activities. Crisis help numbers are always on screen.

Body & EnergyBMI (an adult screening number, not a diagnosis), BMR by Mifflin–St Jeor, daily energy = BMR × FAO/WHO/UNU activity level, plus protein, carb, fat and fibre goals.

Training HallGoal-based sessions with 22 moves and 19 muscle maps; Calisthenics is bodyweight only. “I feel dizzy” and “Chest pain” stop buttons are always at hand.

Running RoadGPS run tracker: distance, moving time, pace and calories (MET × kg × h, 2024 Compendium), with an opt-in Runners’ Board.

Stairs & heart rateLog BPM before, after and one minute after a climb for recovery. Measure pulse with your phone camera (the video is never stored) or pair a smartwatch or chest strap over Web Bluetooth.

EverywhereLog for yesterday or any earlier date, browse every entry by day with totals, and print a weekly report.

PROVE ITScience you can check

Campus Health ObservatoryA 3D map of Kolej MARA Kulim built from OpenStreetMap, in Realistic and Fantasy looks. Every staircase is a spinning heart coloured Mild, Moderate or Vigorous: tap one to log a climb or workout. GPS check-in finds the nearest stairs; your position is never saved or sent.

Stair Sense & Stair SightHeight, work and power from the stairs:
h = n × r   W = m·g·h   P = W ÷ t
Camera edge-finding, and WebXR on Android, measure a real staircase’s rise and tread.

The Group 14 stair experiment Six participants climbed measured stairways at different intensities. The pulse is modelled as periodic motion (frequency, period, angular frequency) and VO₂ is estimated with the project’s own method. Automated tests recompute the report’s tables from the raw counts. If the pulse after is lower than before, the app shows “not valid” instead of a number.

Evidence VaultEvery source on a shelf: source, claim, how HealthWiz uses it and where you see it. Each “?” links straight to its entry.

Science LaboratoryBe Your Own Experiment, the Evidence Trial (reasoning questions built from real data) and the Uncertainty Engine, which shows how unmeasured assumptions move an estimate.

Ascent MonumentYour stair climbing builds an endless Tower of Babel, one stone for every 0.25 m, compared with real landmarks.

Safe dataSaved data is versioned and upgraded step by step; a backup copy is made before any upgrade or repair.
