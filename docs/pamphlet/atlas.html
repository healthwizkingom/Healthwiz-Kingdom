Every part of the kingdom, module by module

Companion to the tri-fold pamphlet. Wellness tracker, not a medical device: estimates are labelled and nothing here diagnoses.

Water & nutrition Provisions Hall

  • Water Quest: a layered valley scene that follows the time of day; the knight walks, drinks, carries and pours; well stages and earlier discoveries.
  • Water goal from body weight (35 mL/kg, the team’s own rule), with extras at higher activity levels.
  • Elemental Thermometer: the water potion adjusts to opt-in live weather (Open-Meteo).
  • Nutrition and hydration on one page; the Kolej MARA Kulim canteen menu with calories; pick several foods and LOG SELECTED once.
  • Add More Food: Luke searches the web (Gemini with Google Search) for any food, portion and change, with a source for each part and an Atwater energy check. Signed out, the canteen menu.
  • Caloric Ledger and Metabolic Forge in Alchemy.
  • Alchemist’s Energy Workshop: food energy, work against gravity and metabolic use are three different quantities; it shows why.
  • Fruit & veg servings (80 g portions, WHO).

Sleep Dream Realm

  • Sleep duration against recommended ranges (National Sleep Foundation); Sleep Score (the team’s own game formula, labelled).
  • Log last night’s sleep in one tap, or for any earlier date.
  • Dream Battle: continuous sleep becomes strength in a torch-lit dungeon with a princess and a deterministic battle.
  • Counting Sheep: a full-screen animated pixel dream.
  • Wake-up alarm: in-app alarm with sounds, snooze and a bedside clock; Android Clock intent; iPhone steps; .ics reminder.
  • A sleep track of its own in the music.

Stress & calm Mind Forest

  • DASS-21 check-in: 21 statements (0–3, past week), three scale scores (× 2, 0–42), shown without severity labels.
  • Storm level from the total; Storm Within: the knight’s face, posture and surroundings follow your rating.
  • Calming Grove: follow a light over the pond as it grows and shrinks with your breath.
  • Relief collection: Shake It Off, Empower Yourself, Arrow Focus, Reaction Focus, Calming Sounds and the original tools.
  • Wizard’s Counsel scene and breathing work signed out; crisis help numbers are shown to everyone.

Body & training Energy Forge

  • BMI (adult screening), healthy weight range, BMR (Mifflin–St Jeor), TDEE (FAO/WHO/UNU activity levels), protein, carbohydrate, fat and fibre goals.
  • “Anything that affects your activity?” question: quest order, stair goals behind a doctor’s note and pace notes follow your answers.
  • Training Hall: goal choice, 22 moves, 19 muscle maps, tutorial, session; Calisthenics is bodyweight only; dizzy and chest-pain stop buttons.
  • Running Road: GPS tracker for distance, moving time, pace and calories; Runners’ Board (opt-in).
  • Haze and heat: live Kulim weather and haze (jerebu) check and a heat tip above Running.

Stairs & heart Stair Mountain · Heartstone Hall

  • Casual stairs (GPS check-in first, then by hand) and workout with heart rate (BPM before, after and 1 minute after).
  • Pulse as periodic motion: f, T and ω; energy and VO₂ by the Group 14 method, behind a “?”; shown as not valid when the pulse after is below the pulse before.
  • Pulse Wave Lab: camera pulse (photoplethysmography) with live wave and BPM; video never stored.
  • Smartwatch or chest strap over Web Bluetooth, plus a “Will my watch work?” sheet.
  • Recovery (1-minute drop), resting-pulse hint, peak heart rate (% of 220 − age, with Tanaka beside it).
  • Stair Prescription: a weekly stair plan you choose (goal, days, minutes); not a prescription.
  • Group 14 measured stairways table (rise, run, hypotenuse, angle).

Stair science Details of a staircase

  • Stair Sense (Gravity Trial): START, PAUSE, FINISH; steps and cadence from the phone’s motion sensor; h = n × r, W = mgh, P = W ÷ t.
  • Stair Sight: 2D step-edge finding in the live camera picture; 3D WebXR (Android Chrome with ARCore) measures rise and tread and builds the stairway; the ghost climbs it.
  • Enchanted Tower, Spectral Pacer, Cadence Echo (microphone), Luna’s Wind Spell and Gravity Anomaly.
  • Dueling Arena: race a replay of your own earlier climb (no XP).
  • Ascent Monument: an endless Tower of Babel from your climbs, one stone per 0.25 m, landmark comparisons, fun facts and a ledger.

Campus Health Observatory the map

  • 3D MapLibre map of Kolej MARA Kulim from OpenStreetMap, in Realistic and Fantasy looks, with clustering markers, search and filters.
  • Staircase stops: a spinning heart on a post in its difficulty colour (Mild, Moderate, Vigorous); solid 3D stops with depth and glow.
  • Game-style HUD: the HealthWiz button menu, search, Legend, a draggable Details sheet (Stairs and Workout tabs), NEAREST STAIRS wave with GPS check-in.
  • Avatars (Tom, anime school boy and girl); a labelled demo walk away from campus; position never saved or sent.
  • Terrain and ground: illustrative three-level terrain with contours; Blender buildings coloured from the drone video; roads, kerbs, pitches and car parks.
  • Sky from live weather, haze and the clock, with a preview button.
  • 17 landmarks with discovery animation, Map journal, Layers panel, sigils for recorded sessions (no XP).
  • Astral Navigator: the Sun’s position (NOAA method) in a compass.
  • 3D stair preview (Three.js) built from survey measurements, labelled illustrative when not surveyed.
  • Survey importer: fill a Word sheet, run one command, refresh.

Kingdom & game Balance Tower

  • Title screen: time of day, live weather, returning-player ribbon and tap reactions; a loading screen fills the HealthWiz heart pixel by pixel.
  • Kingdom map of eight regions in five states up to Flourishing, world progression, a chronicle and the Shadow Keep.
  • Quests: adaptive daily focus quest, weekly quests, Quest Board; Hall of Heroes leaderboard (opt-in, game progress only).
  • Fair XP with a daily allowance and exploration reward; levels with a flowing mana bar; audited badges; gentle streaks with rest days and a week view.
  • Arcana: the Oracle’s crystal ball and the Guild War of Joules (opt-in).
  • Music: a recorded home theme plus tracks for Sleep, Water, Stress, Counsel and the Registry; one switch and volume.
  • Traveller’s Registry onboarding; particle effects for level-ups and badges.

Guides Medius · Luna · Luke

  • Medius keeps the app-wide tour and the Wizard’s Counsel; he reacts to events through speech bubbles with cooldowns.
  • Luna: 12 expression portraits and 21 poses, Meet Luna in Settings, Talk with Luna (AI) and Ask Luna (finds a feature or opens a “?”, no AI).
  • Luke: a small round help button on every Health Hall section opens a spotlight walk-through; he also runs Add More Food.
  • Medius notices: insights from your own baselines and trends, worded as observations, never diagnoses.

Insight & reports Health Hall

  • Health Score (five weighted indicators into 0–100) and the Health Orb with ranked quest cards.
  • Healer’s What-if: try a gentle change; nothing is saved.
  • Health Time Machine: day, week or month timeline of what was really saved.
  • Statistics: touch, keyboard and screen-reader charts; week against week.
  • Weekly Chronicle: a printable report that goes nowhere unless you save it.
  • Log for…: yesterday or any earlier date, never the future; All entries grouped by day with totals and filters.

Science & evidence

  • “?” explanations: inputs, formula, your own numbers step by step, sources and limits.
  • Evidence Vault: every source on shelves, with where you see it; calculation cards open from a “?”.
  • Science Laboratory: Be Your Own Experiment, the Evidence Trial and Royal Surveyors (Truth-Seeker badge).
  • Uncertainty Engine: how unmeasured assumptions move an estimate; no made-up error margins.
  • Group 14 F21 study: raw measurements with every report table worked out from them and tested.

Account, data & platform

  • Install and offline: installable app with a service worker; also a single shareable HTML file.
  • Backup and restore; versioned data with step-by-step upgrades, damaged-data recovery and automatic safety copies.
  • Optional account: email magic link or code, Google sign-in; cloud sync with offline queue, and a choice on first sign-in: keep device, keep cloud or merge.
  • Privacy: Row Level Security, delete account and cloud data, AI keys only on the server.
  • Look and comfort: 8 themes plus custom palettes, High/Balanced/Performance modes, reduced motion, pixel-art emoji, keyboard-friendly dialogs and an error card instead of a blank screen.
  • Quality: 102 browser test files, performance budgets and a device matrix.
